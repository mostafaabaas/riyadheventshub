04 — FAQ

What organisers and brands ask before their first event

What is event and entertainment marketing?

It turns an event from an experience for attendees only into content that lives on social media before, during and after the event — professional coverage, creator content, and audience content (UGC) — so the event and its message reach an audience far larger than the number of people in the venue.

Do you have real experience in the Saudi market?

Yes — influUGC's clients in Saudi Arabia include Riyadh Season and the Saudi Tourism Authority, alongside flynas and Saudia. Riyadh Events Hub is built on exactly that experience: events, entertainment, tourism and aviation — the sectors Riyadh's event calendar runs on.

Do you provide on-ground coverage teams inside Riyadh?

Yes — we coordinate filming and coverage teams on the ground in Riyadh during the event, with content managed moment by moment: short clips published in real time, and wrap-up content (an aftermovie) after the event. Planning, lists and management are handled with the influUGC team well before the event.

What is the difference between event coverage and a UGC campaign for an event?

Coverage is content we produce professionally; a UGC campaign is content creators and the audience produce themselves. Coverage guarantees quality and message; UGC adds credibility and organic spread. Most successful events combine both: our team documents the event, and creators and audiences tell their own experience in their own voice.

How do we start an event or campaign with you?

It starts with a free consultation: book on influugc.com or message us on WhatsApp. We understand the event, its timing and its target audience, then come back with a written plan — coverage, creator lists and proposed activations — before any commitment.

Are your prices published?

No prices are published. Every event is designed around its scale, duration and the coverage and activation it needs, and pricing comes in a written quote after the consultation or brief — no placeholder figures.
